# ✅ Gather Build Checklist — Room 4: Community Headquarters

Build this **fourth**, after [Common Ground](BUILD-CHECKLIST-03-Common-Ground.md). It's the
administrative / leadership / operations space — and the **first room with a private audio
Boardroom**. Keep the **3D prototype's HQ open next to you as the picture** (reception, a workstation
cluster, a records bookshelf, and an enclosed boardroom).

Each line is one action in the Gather **Mapmaker**. Tick them off as you go.

---

## A. Open the builder
- [ ] In the Mapmaker tab, signed in as **Builder/Admin** (client stays **Owner**).

## B. Create the room
- [ ] **Rooms list** → **+ Add a room** → name it exactly **`Community Headquarters`**.
- [ ] Choose **Blank**, about **26 × 18 tiles**.

## C. Floor & walls
- [ ] **Walls & Floors** → **Floors** → **wood** → paint the whole room.
- [ ] Pick a **carpet** floor → paint a zone for the **boardroom** (you'll enclose it in **G**).
- [ ] **Walls** → enclose the room; **leave a 1-tile gap** where the entry portal goes.
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Impassable** → stamp over every wall tile.

## D. Reception & records
- [ ] **Objects** → `reception desk` × 1 — near the entry portal.
- [ ] `bookshelf` × 1 (label it "Records" in your head) — against a wall.
- [ ] `plant` × 2 — soften the corners.

## E. Ops workstation cluster (build ONE, then copy-paste — contract modularity)
- [ ] Place **one** unit: `desk` + `computer`/`monitor` + `office chair`.
- [ ] **Marquee-select** that unit → **Ctrl/Cmd-C** → **Ctrl/Cmd-V** ×3 to make **4 workstations**.
- [ ] **Impassable** over the desks/shelves so avatars don't stand inside the furniture.

## F. Operations board (interactive)
- [ ] **Objects** → `bulletin board` → place it in the open area (not the boardroom).
- [ ] Double-click → **Object Interactions** → **Note**: *"Operations & announcements — latest updates
      for the team."*
- [ ] Add a `link` line / **External link** to the **handbook** URL.

## G. Boardroom — Private Area (important, ID 1)
- [ ] Enclose the carpet zone as a room with `wall`s, leaving a 1-tile doorway.
- [ ] **Objects** → a big meeting `table` + `chair`s around it.
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Private Area** → Name **"Boardroom"**, **ID 1** → **stamp the whole floor**
      of the enclosed room. (Audio/video now stays **inside** for leadership meetings.)
- [ ] **Impassable** over the table.
- [ ] Add a `sign` at the doorway → **Note**: *"Private area — leadership meeting room."*

## H. Portal
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Portal** → stamp the entry tile → destination **Connection Hub** → click its
      exit tile → build the **return** portal in the Hub back to HQ.
- [ ] Place a `door`/`arch` on the portal tile.

## I. Save & test
- [ ] Top nav → **Save**.
- [ ] Portal in from the Hub; confirm walls/desks are solid and the doorway is walkable.
- [ ] Press **X** on the operations board → Note opens, handbook link works.
- [ ] **Test the Boardroom:** walk inside with a second account/tab → audio/video should **stay inside**
      the room and cut off when you step out. (This is the key private-area check.)
