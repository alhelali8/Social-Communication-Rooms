# ✅ Gather Build Checklist — Room 7: Outdoor Community Areas

Build this **seventh**, after [PTI Legacy Hall](BUILD-CHECKLIST-06-PTI-Legacy-Hall.md). It's the
open-air space of walking paths, landscaping, and calm gathering spots — spacious and unhurried. It
also **closes the outdoor loop**: Common Ground ↔ Outdoor ↔ Beach. Keep the **3D prototype's Park &
Outdoors open next to you as the picture**.

Each line is one action in the Gather **Mapmaker**. Tick them off as you go.

---

## A. Open the builder
- [ ] In the Mapmaker tab, signed in as **Builder/Admin** (client stays **Owner**).

## B. Create the room
- [ ] **Rooms list** → **+ Add a room** → name it exactly **`Outdoor Community Areas`**.
- [ ] Choose **Blank**, about **30 × 20 tiles**.

## C. Floor (open and green)
- [ ] **Walls & Floors** → **Floors** → **grass** → paint the whole room.
- [ ] Pick a **stone / dirt path** floor → paint **winding walking paths** linking the three portals.
- [ ] No enclosing walls — this is open community space (low-pressure, no dead-ends).

## D. Landscaping & gathering
Use **Objects** → search each term → click to place:
- [ ] `tree` × 6 and `bush` × 4 and `flower` × 4 — a planted, landscaped feel along the paths.
- [ ] `bench` × 2 and `picnic table` × 1 — spread out, not clustered.
- [ ] `fountain` × 1 — set it as a **gathering circle** (a few benches around it).
- [ ] `lamp` × 1 and `flag` × 1.
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Impassable** → stamp over trees, the fountain, and lamps.

## E. Wayfinding sign (interactive)
- [ ] **Objects** → `signpost` → place at the path junction.
- [ ] Double-click → **Object Interactions** → **Note**: *"Wander the paths and gathering spaces —
      Common Ground is this way, the Beach & Park that way."*

## F. Portals (this room ties the outdoor loop together)
*(Round-trips. Build a `door`/`arch` on each portal tile.)*

**Steps:** **Tile Effects** → **Portal** → stamp tile → destination **Room** → click exit tile →
build the **return** portal back here.
- [ ] Portal ⇄ **Connection Hub**
- [ ] Portal ⇄ **Common Ground**  *(completes Common Ground's pending Outdoor link)*
- [ ] Portal ⇄ **Beach & Park**  *(do when Room 8 is built)*

## G. Save & test
- [ ] Top nav → **Save**.
- [ ] Portal in from the Hub; walk the paths end to end — confirm **no dead-ends** and wide sightlines.
- [ ] Press **X** on the wayfinding sign → Note opens.
- [ ] Walk the loop: Common Ground → Outdoor → (later) Beach → and back. All round-trips should work.
