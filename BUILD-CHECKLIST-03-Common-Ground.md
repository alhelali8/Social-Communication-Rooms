# ✅ Gather Build Checklist — Room 3: Common Ground

Build this **third**, after [Connection Hub](BUILD-CHECKLIST-02-Connection-Hub.md). It's the
central-park **heart** of the universe — relaxed, green, low-pressure, the place people linger.
Keep the **3D prototype's Community Commons open next to you as the picture** (grass, pond, winding
paths, benches in small clusters, a gazebo/stage, the community board).

Each line is one action in the Gather **Mapmaker**. Tick them off as you go.

---

## A. Open the builder
- [ ] In the Mapmaker tab, signed in as **Builder/Admin** (client stays **Owner**).

## B. Create the room
- [ ] **Rooms list** (lower-right) → **+ Add a room** → name it exactly **`Common Ground`**.
- [ ] Choose **Blank**, about **30 × 20 tiles**.

## C. Floor (the park)
- [ ] **Walls & Floors** → **Floors** → pick a **grass** tile → paint the whole room.
- [ ] Pick a **stone path** floor → paint **winding paths** from the entry portal through the space.

## D. The pond
- [ ] **Walls & Floors** → pick a **water** tile → paint a small **pond** off to one side.
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Impassable** → stamp every water tile (avatars walk around, not through).

## E. Objects (cozy, central-park feel)
Use **Objects** → search each term → click to place:
- [ ] `tree` × 6 — scattered, framing the paths (not blocking them).
- [ ] `bush` × 3 and `flower` × 4 — along the path edges and pond.
- [ ] `bench` × 3 and `picnic table` × 2 — see **F** for how to cluster them.
- [ ] `gazebo` (or `stage`) × 1 — a gathering centerpiece.
- [ ] `fountain` × 1 and `lamp` × 2.
- [ ] `coffee cart` (or `cafe` / `food stand`) × 1 — the low-key social anchor.
- [ ] After placing, add **Impassable** tiles over trees, the gazebo, fountain, cart, and lamps.

## F. Relaxed conversation areas
- [ ] Arrange the benches/tables into **2–3 small groupings** (not one big row) — informal networking
      pods where small groups naturally form.
- [ ] Keep wide open sightlines between clusters (low-pressure: no one feels cornered).

## G. Community board (the showcase — interactive)
- [ ] **Objects** → search `bulletin board` → place it beside a main path.
- [ ] Double-click → **Object Interactions** → **Embedded website** → paste your **RSVP Google Form**
      embed URL (`https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/…/viewform?embedded=true`).
- [ ] **Activation distance** = 3. **Custom prompt** = *"Press X to sign up"*.
- [ ] (If the form "refuses to connect," fall back to **Note** + an **External link** to the form.)
- [ ] Low-pressure caption nearby (a `sign` → **Note**): *"Gather, connect, and belong — no pressure,
      just good company."*

## H. Optional extras
- [ ] **Quiet nook (Private Area):** enclose a small bench circle → **Tile Effects** → **Private Area**
      → Name **"Quiet Nook"**, **ID 10** → stamp the floor. (Audio stays inside for 1:1s.)
- [ ] **Spotlight (announcements):** **Tile Effects** → **Spotlight** → stamp the gazebo/stage floor
      (a presenter there is heard room-wide).

## I. Portals
*(Round-trips. Stamp the Hub link now; add the Outdoor link once Room 7 exists.)*

**Steps:** **Tile Effects** → **Portal** → stamp the entry tile → pick destination **Room** → click its
exit tile → build the **return** portal back here. Place a `door`/`arch` on each portal tile.
- [ ] Portal ⇄ **Connection Hub**  *(do now — and finish the Hub-side portal to here)*
- [ ] Portal ⇄ **Outdoor Community Areas**  *(do when Room 7 is built — makes the outdoor loop)*

## J. Save & test
- [ ] Top nav → **Save**.
- [ ] From the Hub, portal into Common Ground; walk the paths; confirm pond/objects are solid.
- [ ] Press **X** on the community board → the **embedded form opens** (submit a test to show success).
- [ ] Step into the Quiet Nook (if built) → confirm the private-area highlight appears.
