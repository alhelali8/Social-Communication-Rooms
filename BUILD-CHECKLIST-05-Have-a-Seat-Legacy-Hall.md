# ✅ Gather Build Checklist — Room 5: Have a Seat Legacy Hall

Build this **fifth**, after [Community Headquarters](BUILD-CHECKLIST-04-Community-Headquarters.md).
It's the founder & mission recognition hall for the **Have a Seat Universe** — grand, museum-like,
reverent. Keep the **3D prototype open as the picture** (pillared entrance, statues on pedestals,
display cases, a mission wall). **Make it visually distinct** from the PTI Legacy Hall (Room 6) so
the two legacies read as separate.

Each line is one action in the Gather **Mapmaker**. Tick them off as you go.

---

## A. Open the builder
- [ ] In the Mapmaker tab, signed in as **Builder/Admin** (client stays **Owner**).

## B. Create the room
- [ ] **Rooms list** → **+ Add a room** → name it exactly **`Have a Seat Legacy Hall`**.
- [ ] Choose **Blank**, about **24 × 16 tiles**.

## C. Floor & walls
- [ ] **Walls & Floors** → **Floors** → **marble / tile** → paint the whole room.
- [ ] Pick a **rug / runner** floor → paint a **central runner** from the entrance to the mission wall.
- [ ] **Walls** → enclose the room; **leave a 1-tile gap** for the entry portal.
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Impassable** → stamp over every wall tile.

## D. Grand entrance
- [ ] **Objects** → `pillar` × 2 — flank the entry portal for a grand approach.
- [ ] **Impassable** over the pillars.

## E. Founder displays (interactive)
- [ ] `statue` (or `bust`) on `pedestal` × 2 — the founders, along the runner.
- [ ] `display case` (or `glass case`) × 3 — artifacts/history, against the side walls.
- [ ] `painting` / `frame` × 1–2 and `bench` × 2 and `plant` × 2 — fill out the gallery feel.
- [ ] **Impassable** over statues, cases, and benches.
- [ ] Double-click **each statue and case** → **Object Interactions** → **Note** → write that
      founder's / artifact's short story. *(One sentence each is fine — keep it readable.)*

## F. Mission & Legacy wall (interactive centerpiece)
- [ ] **Objects** → a large `tv` (or `poster`) on the far wall, at the end of the runner.
- [ ] Double-click → **Object Interactions**:
  - **Embedded website** = a slide deck / recorded tribute, **or**
  - **Note**: *"We preserve the past, enrich the present, and inspire the future."*

## G. Portal
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Portal** → stamp the entry tile → destination **Connection Hub** → click its
      exit tile → build the **return** portal in the Hub back here.
- [ ] Place a `door`/`arch` on the portal tile.

## H. Save & test
- [ ] Top nav → **Save**.
- [ ] Portal in from the Hub; confirm the runner leads the eye to the mission wall.
- [ ] Press **X** on a founder statue and the mission wall → the Notes / embed open.
