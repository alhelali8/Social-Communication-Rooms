# ✅ Gather Build Checklist — Room 6: PTI Legacy Hall

Build this **sixth**, after [Have a Seat Legacy Hall](BUILD-CHECKLIST-05-Have-a-Seat-Legacy-Hall.md).
It's the distinct legacy/recognition hall for the **Paper Tole Institute (PTI)** — its own mission and
history, kept **visually separate** from Room 5 so the two legacies clearly read as two institutions.
Keep the **3D prototype open as the picture** (framed paper-tole artworks, cases, the PTI founder, a
mission/history board).

Each line is one action in the Gather **Mapmaker**. Tick them off as you go.

> Keep it distinct from Room 5: different **wall/floor color**, **artwork-forward** (framed pieces on
> walls) rather than statue-forward, and PTI's own signage tone.

---

## A. Open the builder
- [ ] In the Mapmaker tab, signed in as **Builder/Admin** (client stays **Owner**).

## B. Create the room
- [ ] **Rooms list** → **+ Add a room** → name it exactly **`PTI Legacy Hall`**.
- [ ] Choose **Blank**, about **24 × 16 tiles**.

## C. Floor & walls
- [ ] **Walls & Floors** → **Floors** → **marble / tile** (pick a **different tone** than Room 5).
- [ ] Pick a **rug / runner** floor → paint a central runner.
- [ ] **Walls** → enclose the room; **leave a 1-tile gap** for the entry portal.
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Impassable** → stamp over every wall tile.

## D. Entrance
- [ ] **Objects** → `pillar` × 2 — flank the entry portal.
- [ ] **Impassable** over the pillars.

## E. PTI artworks (interactive — the focus of this hall)
- [ ] `frame` (or `painting`) × 4 — mounted along the walls = the **paper-tole artworks**.
- [ ] `display case` × 2 — protected pieces / tools of the craft.
- [ ] `statue` × 1 — the **PTI founder**.
- [ ] `bench` × 1 and `plant` × 2 — gallery comfort.
- [ ] **Impassable** over frames-at-floor, cases, statue, and bench.
- [ ] Double-click **each frame** → **Object Interactions** → **Note** describing that piece and the
      **paper-tole craft** (what it is, how it's made).

## F. PTI Mission & History (interactive)
- [ ] **Objects** → a `bulletin board` (or `tv`) on a feature wall.
- [ ] Double-click → **Object Interactions**:
  - **Note** with PTI's mission + a short history, **or**
  - **Embedded website** = a PTI slide/page if you have one.

## G. Portal
- [ ] **Tile Effects** → **Portal** → stamp the entry tile → destination **Connection Hub** → click its
      exit tile → build the **return** portal in the Hub back here.
- [ ] Place a `door`/`arch` on the portal tile.

## H. Save & test
- [ ] Top nav → **Save**.
- [ ] Portal in from the Hub; confirm it **looks different** from the Have a Seat hall at a glance.
- [ ] Press **X** on an artwork frame and the mission board → Notes / embed open.
